# Quantara 0.9.0 Strategy Validation Preview

## Release intent

This release turns the earlier 0.6–0.9 roadmap into a device-testable
vertical slice. It remains a read-only research and paper-simulation
application. It cannot submit, modify, or cancel a real exchange order.

## Included

### 0.6 Market Context

- deterministic market-regime classification:
  - accumulation;
  - markup;
  - distribution;
  - markdown;
  - range;
  - transition / uncertain;
- regime is reported beside Strategy Lab results;
- all decisions continue to use closed candles only.

### 0.7 Strategy Modules

- `structure-zones` v1.1;
- `trend-candle-continuation` v0.1 research;
- `dow-swing-continuation` v0.1 research;
- `kbsm-weekly-shadow` v0.1 shadow;
- explicit strategy maturity and supported-timeframe boundaries;
- independent symbol, timeframe, strategy, and test-window selection.

### 0.8 Risk and Trade Management

- normal per-setup risk control is capped at 2%;
- Strategy Lab caps paper-test risk at 1%;
- position sizing continues to derive from stop distance and estimated costs;
- simulated partial exits use TP1, TP2, and TP3;
- fees and adverse slippage are included;
- ambiguous intrabar SL/TP ordering is resolved conservatively as stop first.

### 0.9 Validation

- deterministic point-in-time historical replay without future candle access;
- persistent forward-paper sessions on Android;
- forward-session limits that keep the complete result recoverable from the
  current market-data window:
  - 15m: up to 1 day;
  - 1h: up to 3 days;
  - 4h: up to 7 days;
- net P&L and return;
- trade, win, loss, and stop counts;
- TP1, TP2, and TP3 touch counts;
- win rate;
- expectancy;
- profit factor;
- maximum drawdown;
- small-sample and experimental-strategy warnings.

## Important validation limits

- The device preview does not yet have point-in-time funding history,
  exchange maintenance-margin tiers, liquidation calculations, order-book
  liquidity, latency, or partial-fill evidence.
- Dominance and relative-strength history are not yet available in the client
  validation dataset.
- Walk-forward parameter optimization and strict out-of-sample promotion stay
  in the backend research boundary; the client does not label a strategy as
  profitable or approved.
- A forward-paper session catches up from closed public candles when the app
  is reopened. It does not run a permanent Android background service.
- KBSM and the two new continuation modules remain research-only or
  experimental. Their presence is not evidence of profitability.

## Safety boundary

No API key, private account access, real-money order placement, withdrawal,
autonomous execution, or profit guarantee is included.

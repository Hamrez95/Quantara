# Quantara 0.10.2 Android cold-start recovery

## Incident

The `0.10.1-preview` APK installed successfully but the application process closed immediately on a Samsung SM-A556E running Android 16. Android displayed the generic message that Quantara closed because the app had a bug.

## Hotfix boundary

This candidate prioritizes a reliable first frame and a testable foreground application:

- starts Flutter immediately without awaiting optional background services;
- launches through a minimal `SafeMainActivity` with no custom WebView platform view or native method-channel registration;
- removes WorkManager and Flutter local-notification plugins from the launch-critical plugin graph;
- keeps foreground market scanning, paper signal generation, journaling, risk sizing, strategy lab, and the pure-Flutter chart;
- temporarily disables periodic background scans and background setup notifications;
- bumps the preview to `0.10.2+10`.

Theme/language persistence and the legacy native foreground notification bridge are also outside the safe launch activity. They may be restored incrementally after physical-device launch acceptance.

## New release gates

The Flutter workflow now:

1. pins Flutter `3.44.8`;
2. runs formatting, analyzer, and all tests;
3. builds universal and `arm64-v8a` release APKs;
4. validates both APKs with 16 KB ZIP-alignment checks;
5. installs the universal APK on an Android API 36 emulator;
6. cold-starts `SafeMainActivity`, verifies that the process remains alive, and rejects fatal logcat evidence.

## Promotion policy

This is a crash-recovery candidate, not a stable release. Do not merge to `dev`, tag, or promote to `main` until:

- CI is green;
- the API 36 launch artifact is produced;
- the APK launches successfully on the reported Samsung Android 16 device;
- foreground scan, navigation, charts, signal journal, and strategy lab receive a brief device smoke test.

Closes the launch blocker tracked by issue #50 only after physical-device confirmation.

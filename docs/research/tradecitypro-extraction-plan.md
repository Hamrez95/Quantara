# Trade City Pro extraction plan

## Objective

Use product-owner-approved Trade City Pro videos to generate measurable research hypotheses while respecting creator rights and YouTube platform policies. The workflow stores metadata, timestamps, evidence links, and original Quantara specifications. It does not scrape or archive videos, subtitles, or full transcripts.

## Source role

Registry source: `tradecitypro-youtube-channel`

Decision role: `hypothesis_only`

Trade City Pro is selected because its educational style aligns with the product owner's preferred way of understanding charts and market narratives. Preference is a product-design input, not statistical evidence.

## Selection workflow

1. The product owner or research curator selects a specific video or playlist.
2. Quantara records only documented YouTube API metadata or manually supplied identifiers:
   - channel ID;
   - playlist ID when applicable;
   - video ID;
   - title;
   - publication time;
   - duration;
   - canonical URL;
   - selection reason;
   - reviewer identity and review time.
3. A human watches the source through the official YouTube experience.
4. The reviewer records timestamped observations in original wording.
5. An LLM may transform those observations into a strict candidate-rule schema.
6. A second pass challenges ambiguity, hindsight, repainting, hidden discretion, and non-measurable language.
7. Only a deterministic specification enters strategy research.

## Prohibited collection

- no page scraping;
- no media download;
- no subtitle or transcript scraping;
- no full transcript storage even if manually obtained;
- no automated bulk channel ingestion;
- no copied charts, paid material, or substantial excerpts;
- no automated comments, likes, views, subscriptions, or other engagement;
- no use of undocumented YouTube endpoints.

If the creator provides written permission for a broader use, the permission record must be reviewed and a new `user_supplied_licensed` registry entry created. Existing policy is not silently widened.

## Candidate-rule schema

Each extracted hypothesis must contain:

- `candidate_id`;
- `source_id`;
- `video_id` and canonical URL;
- one or more evidence timestamps;
- original Quantara paraphrase;
- market and instrument scope;
- allowed timeframes;
- required input data;
- market-regime preconditions;
- precise setup conditions;
- entry trigger;
- invalidation condition;
- stop methodology;
- target or exit methodology;
- no-trade conditions;
- maximum information availability time for every input;
- unresolved ambiguities;
- falsification tests;
- authoring model/prompt version when applicable;
- human reviewer and approval state.

The schema never contains an `execute`, `place_order`, or unrestricted natural-language instruction field.

## Ambiguity challenge

The reviewer must translate terms such as the following into measurable definitions:

- strong support;
- weak resistance;
- good volume;
- trend is healthy;
- divergence is meaningful;
- liquidity was taken;
- breakout is confirmed;
- market looks bullish;
- news is positive.

For each phrase, the specification must define observable inputs, thresholds, timing, and invalidation. If two reviewers cannot reproduce the classification from the same closed data, the rule remains `ambiguous` and cannot be backtested as an approved strategy.

## Anti-hindsight controls

- Observations are timestamped to information available at the simulated decision time.
- Higher-timeframe values must come from closed bars unless explicitly modeled otherwise.
- News and macro facts use actual publication time, not event date alone.
- Visual zones must be generated by a deterministic algorithm or frozen before future bars are revealed.
- Repainting indicators and discretionary redraws are rejected or separately modeled as human annotations.
- A later explanation cannot modify the original candidate without creating a new version.

## Strategy research path

A candidate moves through these states:

1. `captured`
2. `structured`
3. `ambiguous` or `testable`
4. `implemented`
5. `in_sample_rejected` or `validation_pending`
6. `unseen_data_rejected` or `paper_candidate`
7. `paper_rejected` or `shadow_candidate`
8. `shadow_rejected` or `promotion_review`

No state transition is based on narrative confidence alone. Every transition records immutable evidence and the exact strategy version.

## Evaluation requirements

At minimum:

- multiple non-overlapping periods;
- BTC and ETH before broad altcoin generalization;
- bull, bear, range, high-volatility, and low-liquidity regimes;
- realistic spread, slippage, fee, funding, latency, and partial fills;
- parameter sensitivity rather than one best setting;
- comparison with simple baselines;
- out-of-sample and walk-forward evidence;
- maximum drawdown, expectancy, profit factor, calibration, and stability beside win rate;
- paper and shadow reconciliation before any execution discussion.

## User experience output

When a Trade City Pro-inspired idea appears in Quantara, the UI should show:

- the source and evidence link;
- that it is a creator-derived hypothesis;
- the deterministic conditions currently satisfied or unsatisfied;
- the market regime;
- uncertainty and contradictory evidence;
- invalidation, stop, target, and risk budget;
- historical evidence with costs and unseen-data separation;
- a clear distinction among educational analysis, research signal, paper decision, shadow decision, and any future restricted live action.

The creator's name must never be used to imply endorsement, partnership, or guaranteed performance.

## Initial curation request

The product owner can later provide a short list of preferred videos or playlists. Until then, Quantara records the official channel as an approved discovery source but does not bulk-ingest its catalog.
